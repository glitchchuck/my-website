Auditing in Nepal: Navigating Challenges, Unlocking Opportunities

Critical insights into your financial health and operational integrity.

Common Audit Findings in Nepal

  • Inadequate internal controls leading to fraud or errors.
  • Non-compliance with local tax laws and regulations.
  • Lack of proper documentation for financial transactions.
  • Under-reporting or misclassification of income and expenses.
  • Weak asset management and inventory control systems.

Why Low Involvement in Auditing?

  • Lack of Awareness: Limited understanding of long-term benefits.
  • Perceived Cost: Viewing auditing as an expense rather than an investment.
  • Limited Capacity: Shortage of qualified and experienced auditors.
  • Informal Practices: Prevalence of informal financial practices.
